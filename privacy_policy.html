Privacy Policy

MotorSonic / MotorSonar Application
Effective Date: August 2, 2026

Summary: MotorSonic values your privacy. We process audio recordings locally and securely solely for motor/engine diagnostic analysis. We do not sell your personal data or engine audio files to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

To provide engine sound diagnostics and vehicle health monitoring, MotorSonic collects the following types of information:

  • Audio Data (Microphone): When you perform an engine sound scan, MotorSonic accesses your device microphone to capture acoustic audio signals of your vehicle engine. Audio processing for frequency spectrum and pattern recognition is performed locally or securely processed for diagnostic output.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Technical details such as device model, OS version, app performance logs, and anonymized crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics) to ensure stability and improve diagnosis accuracy.
  • Vehicle Scan Records: Engine scan history, diagnostic trouble logs, frequency metrics, and user notes saved locally on your device or linked to your anonymous user ID.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Analyzing engine sound signatures to detect mechanical anomalies, misfires, bearing noise, or exhaust leaks.
  • Generating detailed diagnostic insights and plain-English repair suggestions via Google AI / Gemini API integration.
  • Maintaining and optimizing application stability, performance, and features.
  • Saving your historical scan reports for personal vehicle tracking.

3. Permissions Required

MotorSonic requests specific device permissions to operate core features:

  • RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone Access): Required to record and process engine acoustic signals during active diagnostic scans. Microphone access is active ONLY while performing a manual scan.
  • INTERNET & NETWORK STATE: Required to query Google AI diagnostic endpoints and sync crash/performance telemetry.
  • READ/WRITE EXTERNAL STORAGE (Optional): Required if you export PDF diagnostic reports or save engine sound logs to your device storage.

4. Third-Party Services

We partner with trusted service providers to deliver robust AI analysis and app reliability. These services handle data under strict privacy standards:

  • Google Cloud & Vertex AI / Gemini API: Used to generate intelligent diagnostic explanations from processed frequency metrics and sound parameters.
  • Google Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Used for anonymized app performance telemetry, error reporting, and operational diagnostics.

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your information:

  • Your scan logs and diagnostic history are primarily stored locally on your device.
  • Audio recordings captured during scans are processed transiently and are not permanently retained on external servers unless saved locally by you.
  • You can clear app cache and data or uninstall the application at any time to remove locally stored scan history.

6. Children's Privacy

MotorSonic is designed as a vehicle diagnostic utility and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be updated on this page with a revised effective date.
